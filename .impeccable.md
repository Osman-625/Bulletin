# Bulletin — Impeccable Design Context

## Design Context

### Users

INTI International University community — students, staff, faculty, and club officers. The public feed is browsed without login by anyone on campus, on any device. Students scan between classes on mobile; staff check announcements at the start of the day on desktop. Publishers (admins, club officers, university comms) create content via the dashboard.

Announcements come from both official university sources and student-run clubs — the UI must serve both voices without privileging one over the other.

### Brand Personality

**Three words: warm, spirited, legitimate.**

The interface should feel like a real campus gathering space — communal, friendly, lived-in — while carrying the energy of student event posters AND the authority of official university announcements. Not trying to be cool. Not trying to be corporate. Genuinely _of_ the campus.

### Aesthetic Direction

**Editorial campus board** — the spiritual ancestor is a well-curated physical noticeboard: layered, color-coded, typographically intentional, human.

**Current design system (implemented):**

- **Palette (Future Commons):** Warm amber/cream canvas `#F7F3E7`, INTI Red accent `#C8102E` / `#d91234`, navy `#14355F`, gold `#D4941F`. Dark mode: near-black `#15110f` canvas, brighter INTI Red `#e8203a`. OKLCH throughout.
- **Typography:** Atkinson Hyperlegible (body/sans) + Bricolage Grotesque (display) + Geist Mono (code)
- **Layout:** Bento grid (dense auto-flow, col/row span variants) for the public feed
- **Components:** GlassCard, card-lift/action-lift interactions, BrandMark with layered glow
- **Theme:** OS-preference driven — both light and dark are first-class; no opinionated default

**Anti-references (explicit do-not-look-like):**

- Generic SaaS (Stripe / Linear / Notion) — neutral, impersonal, corporate
- Legacy university portals (MyINTI, Moodle) — cluttered, institutional grays, small type
- Social media feeds (Instagram / Facebook) — infinite scroll, dopamine loops, engagement bait
- Generic AI UI — cyan accents, purple-to-blue gradients, glowing glass on dark backgrounds

### Design Principles

1. **Content first.** Announcements are the product. UI chrome recedes; cards lead. No decorative elements that compete with the content itself.
2. **Human over corporate.** Made by people for people — warmer, more alive than a software company's idea of a campus board.
3. **Scannable at a glance.** Campus users move fast. Strong typographic hierarchy, clear category signals, nothing buried. The most important thing on any card reads in under a second.
4. **Both voices fit.** A polished university-wide announcement and a student club recruitment post should coexist without one looking out of place next to the other. Category/status signals distinguish them, not visual register.
5. **Not social media.** No algorithmic pressure, no engagement metrics, no infinite scroll urgency. Calm, unhurried, respectful of attention.
