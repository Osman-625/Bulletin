# Bulletin — Impeccable Design Context

## Design Context

### Users
Mixed audience: university students and staff at INTI International University (Malaysia). Used throughout the day on both mobile and desktop. Students glance at the feed between classes; staff publishers manage content from a dashboard. The public feed is the primary touchpoint — must communicate urgency and hierarchy at a glance.

### Brand Personality
**Three words: Editorial, Refined, Readable.**
Not a social app. Not a startup SaaS. A well-run institution communicating clearly — authority without coldness. Think a well-typeset campus broadsheet, not a marketing website or admin portal.

### Aesthetic Direction
- **Theme:** Light mode primary (browsed in daylight across campus), dark mode supported
- **Palette:** Warm beige canvas (#F7F5F2), INTI Red (#C8102E) as dominant accent, Navy (#002147) and Gold (#D4941F) as supporting brand colors
- **Feel:** Warm, institutional, typographically serious. Not trendy. Not cold.
- **Anti-references:** MyINTI portal (dated, cluttered), social media feeds (too casual), startup SaaS gradient aesthetic (impersonal, generic)
- **Differentiation:** The interface should feel made *for* this institution — not a template skinned in INTI colors. Editorial hierarchy, announcement content as king.

### Design Principles
1. **Content over chrome** — The announcement is the product. UI elements should recede; text and imagery lead.
2. **Institutional without being institutional** — Authority and trust without coldness. Warm palette, refined typography.
3. **Clarity at a glance** — Mixed audience, all day. Urgency, category, and recency must read in under 2 seconds.
4. **Earn every effect** — Glassmorphism, shadows, and animations only when they serve content hierarchy, not as decoration.
5. **Typography is the design** — For an announcement-driven product, the typeface choice and scale ARE the brand.

### Users
**Public (students)**: Browse, filter, and read university announcements on mobile or desktop — casual, quick sessions, often on-the-go. They want to find what's relevant to them fast.

**Publishers (staff/admins)**: Create, draft, edit, and publish announcements on behalf of INTI clubs, departments, or student organizations. Desktop-primary, task-focused sessions.

### Brand Personality
**Warm · Modern · Clear**

Bulletin is the official voice of INTI International University — authoritative enough to be trusted, warm enough to feel welcoming, modern enough to not feel like a bureaucratic portal. It should feel like a well-designed student newspaper app, not a government notice board and not a generic SaaS dashboard.

### Aesthetic Direction
- **Theme**: Light-primary with full dark mode. Day-use university context → light default.
- **Typography**: Playfair Display for editorial headings (display/hero), DM Sans for UI and body.
- **Colors**: INTI red `#C8102E` as accent, deep navy `#002147`, warm gold `#D4941F`. Warm off-white canvas `#FAF9F7` — never pure white.
- **Motion**: Purposeful and delightful. Staggered fade-ups on page load, smooth transitions. Feels alive without being distracting.
- **Feel**: Editorial magazine meets modern app. Think NYT app crossed with a well-crafted university portal.

### Anti-Reference
- **NOT**: Old-web table layouts, cluttered sidebars, banner-heavy portal pages (i.e. no "MyINTI" portal energy)
- **NOT**: Generic dark SaaS dashboard with cyan/purple neon accents

### Design Principles
1. **Warm editorial over cold corporate** — Typography-led hierarchy, generous whitespace, readable prose
2. **Motion earns its place** — Every animation serves orientation or delight, never decoration
3. **Mobile-first, not mobile-afterthought** — Public feed must be excellent on phone; dashboard must work on tablet
4. **Tokens everywhere** — No hard-coded colors or spacing in component code; all values from the design system
5. **WCAG AA minimum** — Contrast ratios ≥ 4.5:1 for normal text, interactive elements properly labeled
