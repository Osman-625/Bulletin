# Bulletin - Impeccable Design Context

Last refreshed from source: 2026-06-05.

## Design Context

### Users

Bulletin serves the INTI International University community: students, staff,
faculty, club officers, publishers, admins, super-admins, and owners.

The public board is browsed without login by students and staff on any device.
Students are usually mobile, moving between classes, and scanning quickly for
urgency, category, date, and relevance. Staff and faculty often check the board
from desktop at the start of the day. Publishers and role-based administrators
use dashboard workflows to create, review, manage, and govern announcements.

Official university announcements and student-run club posts must coexist. The
interface should give both voices a legitimate place without turning the product
into a social feed.

### Brand Personality

Warm, spirited, legitimate.

Bulletin should feel like a real campus gathering space: communal, clear,
helpful, and alive. It should carry the authority of an official university
notice while still having the energy of student posters and campus life. It
should not feel like generic SaaS, social media, a legacy university portal, or
AI-generated marketing UI.

### Aesthetic Direction

Future Commons is an editorial campus board: a well-curated physical noticeboard
translated into a responsive product. The design is typography-led, warm, dense
when useful, and restrained enough that announcement content stays primary.

Current source-of-truth implementation:

- Stack: Next.js 16.2.6 App Router, React 19.2.4, TypeScript, Tailwind CSS v4,
  shadcn, Supabase, Vercel.
- Tokens: `src/app/globals.css` is canonical for color, radius, shadows,
  spacing-like utilities, motion, and theme behavior.
- Typography: `src/app/layout.tsx` wires Atkinson Hyperlegible to `--font-sans`,
  Bricolage Grotesque to `--font-newsreader` and then `--font-display`, and
  Geist Mono to `--font-mono`.
- Theme: light and dark are both first-class through `next-themes`; do not design
  a one-mode-only experience.
- Public board: `src/app/(public)/page.tsx`,
  `src/components/announcements/AnnouncementCard.tsx`, and
  `src/components/public/*` build a responsive editorial board using
  `future-bento-cell`, `public-board-*`, `dense-bento-board`,
  `uniform-card-board`, and `priority-lane-board`.
- Dashboard: role-group layouts use `DashboardShell`, `DashboardSidebar`,
  `DashboardTopNav`, and `GlassCard` from `src/components/dashboard`.

### Design Principles

1. **Content first.** Announcements are the product. UI chrome supports the
   announcement title, summary, category, date, image, deadline, and action.
2. **Scannable at a glance.** Campus users move fast. Urgency, category, recency,
   and relevance should be readable in roughly two seconds.
3. **Human over corporate.** The interface should feel made for campus life, not
   reskinned from a startup dashboard or a legacy admin portal.
4. **Both voices fit.** Official notices and student club posts should sit in the
   same system without one looking visually wrong.
5. **Mobile-primary public board.** Public browsing must be excellent at phone
   widths. Dashboard workflows should remain usable at tablet widths.
6. **Tokens everywhere.** Use CSS variables from `globals.css`; do not hard-code
   hex colors in components.
7. **Accessible by default.** Keep touch targets at least 44px, meet WCAG AA
   contrast for body text, preserve visible focus states, and respect
   `prefers-reduced-motion`.

### Guardrails

- Do not create `src/middleware.ts`; the auth gateway is `src/proxy.ts`.
- Do not bypass role checks in server actions. RLS, `src/proxy.ts`, and action
  authorization must all hold.
- Do not expose service-role Supabase clients or `SUPABASE_SERVICE_ROLE_KEY` to
  client components.
- Do not hand-write database row shapes; use `src/lib/database.types.ts`.
- Do not format announcement dates ad hoc in components; use
  `src/lib/announcements/public-board.ts` and `Asia/Kuala_Lumpur`.
- Avoid social engagement patterns, infinite-scroll pressure, generic SaaS
  metric dashboards, purple-blue gradients, decorative glass/glow, and nested
  cards.
- Prefer existing components and primitives before adding new abstractions.
