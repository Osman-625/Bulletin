# Bulletin Brand Directions

Date: 2026-05-14

This document defines three fresh branding directions for Bulletin. The goal is not to copy INTI International University, but to build a product identity that feels credible inside INTI's ecosystem while giving Bulletin its own memorable visual language.

The visual comparison route lives at `/brand-lab`.

## Research Summary

### INTI Brand And Digital Experience

INTI's public site presents itself as a structured academic institution: programme-led navigation, campus pathways, news, events, international-student content, and direct request-info actions. The experience relies on institutional clarity rather than playful product storytelling.

Key cues to adapt:

- **Red authority signal:** INTI's red is the strongest recognition cue. Bulletin should preserve red as the primary action and urgency color.
- **Academic structure:** The site organizes around programmes, campuses, admissions, events, and news. Bulletin should echo that discipline through clear sections, metadata, and hierarchy.
- **Campus credibility:** Photography and event/news framing make the university feel active. Bulletin should treat posters and announcement images as real campus artifacts, not decorative thumbnails.
- **Direct action paths:** INTI's site repeatedly routes users toward enquiry, programme, event, and contact actions. Bulletin should always make "what next" obvious.
- **Formal tone:** The voice is institutional, informative, and confident. Bulletin can be warmer, but should avoid social-feed looseness.

### 2026 UI Direction

The strongest current direction for university platforms is not trend-heavy novelty. The useful trends are the ones that improve scannability, governance, and trust:

- **Editorial information architecture:** Better content models, clearer hierarchy, and fewer decorative sections.
- **Warm tactile surfaces:** Paper, boards, stamps, and human texture are useful counters to generic AI polish.
- **Bento, but purposeful:** Modular layouts work when they expose priority, urgency, date, location, and ownership.
- **Kinetic typography with restraint:** Motion should orient users through page load, filtering, and status changes.
- **Poster-native feeds:** University announcements often arrive as posters. The design system must preserve aspect ratio and readability.
- **Accessibility as style:** Focus states, target size, contrast, reduced motion, and readable type should be visible parts of the brand.
- **Responsive role shift:** Mobile is for scanning and reading. Desktop is for publishing, review, and governance.

## Shared Brand Rules

- Bulletin is **INTI-inspired, not INTI-branded**. Do not redraw or imitate the official INTI logo.
- Red remains the authority signal, but it should be used with restraint.
- Warm neutrals replace generic white/gray dashboards.
- Navy is institutional depth. Gold is deadline, date, issue, and value metadata.
- Posters are content, not decoration. They should be shown uncropped whenever possible.
- Motion must explain state or hierarchy: page load, filter confirmation, hover/press feedback, loading.
- Dark mode is supported, but light mode is the primary campus-day experience.
- UI text should feel official and short. No marketing filler.

## Direction 1: Campus Press

**Role:** The credibility direction  
**Personality:** Editorial, credible, measured  
**Tagline:** A campus broadsheet rebuilt for a fast university feed.

Campus Press is closest to the academic institution. It makes Bulletin feel like a curated editorial board: issue numbers, date stamps, clear sections, strict metadata, and warm paper surfaces. It is the safest direction for stakeholder approval because it immediately reads as official.

### Typography

- Display: Literata
- Body: Atkinson Hyperlegible
- Labels: Geist Mono

Literata gives academic editorial weight without becoming antique. Atkinson keeps long announcements readable and inclusive.

### Color

- Canvas: `#F3EEE4`
- Surface: `#FFF9EF`
- Ink: `#211713`
- INTI-inspired red: `#C8102E`
- Navy: `#002147`
- Gold: `#C98714`
- Line: `#D8C9B8`

### Layout

Broadsheet bento:

- One dominant lead announcement
- Secondary editorial rail
- Compact metadata strips
- Clear date/category stamps
- Dashboard as an editorial queue

### Motion

Paper-settling motion:

- Masthead appears first
- Issue rule draws in
- Feed modules fade/lift in a short stagger
- Hover presses cards by 1px and sharpens metadata
- Reduced motion uses opacity only

### Component Style

- Navigation: compact masthead with issue/date line
- Cards: editorial cells with full borders and date stamps
- Filters: newspaper-section tabs
- Forms: grouped by content, audience, schedule, proof
- Iconography: stamps, issue marks, small line icons

### Announcement/Poster Style

Posters sit inside a warm paper mat and remain uncropped. Text hierarchy remains dominant, with posters treated like inserted print artifacts.

### Best Fit

Use Campus Press if stakeholders want the safest, most official direction.

## Direction 2: Signal Atlas

**Role:** The speed direction  
**Personality:** Directional, fast, precise  
**Tagline:** Announcements as campus signals, routes, and live waypoints.

Signal Atlas turns announcement metadata into wayfinding. It is the most product-like direction and works well for deadlines, locations, multi-campus content, and fast mobile scanning.

### Typography

- Display: Archivo Narrow
- Body: Atkinson Hyperlegible
- Labels: Geist Mono

Archivo Narrow feels like signage and route systems. It compresses well for metadata-heavy UI.

### Color

- Canvas: `#EEF2EF`
- Surface: `#FBF8EF`
- Ink: `#111C22`
- INTI-inspired red: `#C8102E`
- Navy: `#002147`
- Gold: `#D4941F`
- Line: `#CBD5D6`

### Layout

Route-grid composition:

- Left route rail
- Waypoints for deadlines and locations
- Compact status modules
- Filter controls as map legends
- Dashboard as a signal board

### Motion

Signal motion:

- A red signal travels across the route line on load
- Waypoints pulse once on selection
- Filters behave like route toggles
- Reduced motion uses static route lines and opacity-only changes

### Component Style

- Navigation: destination labels and compact search
- Cards: waypoint cards with date, location, urgency, and action
- Filters: map legend controls
- Forms: publishing flow as a status route
- Iconography: geometric route lines, dots, pins, LEDs

### Announcement/Poster Style

Posters become destination placards paired with date/location coordinates.

### Best Fit

Use Signal Atlas if the product should prioritize deadlines, speed, and multi-campus navigation.

## Direction 3: Future Commons

**Role:** The student energy direction  
**Personality:** Warm, open, energetic  
**Tagline:** A warmer campus commons for notices, clubs, and opportunity.

Future Commons is the recommended production direction. It keeps INTI authority while making Bulletin feel like a living campus board. It is student-facing enough for clubs, workshops, careers, and showcases, but still official enough for staff publishing and institutional announcements.

### Typography

- Display: Bricolage Grotesque
- Body: Atkinson Hyperlegible
- Labels: Faculty Glyphic

Bricolage gives warmth and personality. Atkinson preserves accessibility. Faculty Glyphic adds a distinct handmade label voice for small metadata moments.

### Color

- Canvas: `#F8F2DF`
- Surface: `#FFFAF0`
- Ink: `#241815`
- INTI-inspired red: `#C8102E`
- Navy: `#143A5F`
- Gold: `#D4941F`
- Line: `#E2D3BD`
- Dark canvas: `#15110F`

### Layout

Commons-board modules:

- One strong featured announcement
- Poster-native cards
- Offset clusters and board rhythm
- Clear category chips
- Dashboard as a publishing board

### Motion

Friendly but disciplined:

- Board builds in layers
- Posters reveal after masthead
- Cards lift slightly on hover
- Category chips clarify selected state
- Reduced motion uses opacity and static modules

### Component Style

- Navigation: warm official masthead with red action
- Cards: poster-first announcement cards, modest radius, strong text
- Filters: segmented category controls
- Forms: preview beside schedule/audience controls
- Dashboard: warm operational board, not generic SaaS glass
- Iconography: compass/signal motifs, rounded line icons, category swatches

### Announcement/Poster Style

Posters are first-class artifacts. They should use `object-contain`, preserve aspect ratio, and be paired with concise metadata.

### Best Fit

Use Future Commons as the production direction unless the project must feel more conservative for stakeholder review.

## Recommendation

Select **Future Commons** for production.

Reasons:

- The current project already leans toward warm campus-board tokens.
- It best supports real announcement posters without cropping.
- It has enough student-life personality to avoid feeling like a generic admin portal.
- It still preserves INTI-inspired red, institutional hierarchy, and official tone.
- It can absorb Campus Press editorial rules for formal notices and Signal Atlas urgency rules for deadlines.
